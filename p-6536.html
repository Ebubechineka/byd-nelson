DYNAMIC FWD

The BYD SEALION 8 is available in three model variants. The Dynamic FWD variant features a 12.9kWh battery, delivering an impressive balance of efficiency and performance.

The SEALION 8 Dynamic Front-Wheel Drive is powered by BYD’s advanced Xiaoyun 1.5-litre high-efficiency plug-in hybrid engine, offering an all-electric driving range of up to 103km (NEDC) and a combined range of up to 1118km.

Paired with a single front-mounted electric motor producing 205kW of power and 315Nm of torque, and supported by a 60-litre fuel tank, the SEALION 8 Dynamic FWD accelerates from 0–100km/h in just 8.6 seconds.

Super Hybrid Your Way

The BYD SEALION 8 offers two flavours of Super Hybrid – DM-i for efficient and intelligent driving or DM-p for the ultimate performance. Both powertrains use the latest generation of BYD’s Dual-Mode 5.0 technology, with 1.5-litre turbocharged engine that is one of the most thermally efficient in the world.

Technology That Pampers

Sink into the SEALION 8 and find yourself in extreme comfort. The Premium model treats you with genuine leather seats – that offer heating, ventilation and massaging in the first and second row. The luxury car experience is completed with a full length panoramic sunroof (all models) and 21 speaker premium sound system (Premium model only).

The Very Latest from BYD

As BYD’s flagship SUV, the SEALION 8 has the very latest in car technology. The 26-inch ultra-wide heads-up display, 10.25-inch digital driver’s display, and 15.6-inch infotainment screen all run the latest operating system with intuitive controls and ultra-smart voice recognition. It’s also the first BYD in New Zealand to offer Nap, Camping, and Car Wash mode.

The Luxury of Space

Step inside the SEALION 8 and you’ll find a cabin that is luxuriously appointed, spacious and high tech (it is a BYD, after all). Whether you need three rows of seating or 1,960 litres of boot space, the SEALION 8 offers unrivalled versatility.

21 Speaker surround sound

The SEALION 8 premium immerses your sense with a upgraded premium sound system, boasting 21 speakers for a high-definition sound matter where you’re seated.

HEADS UP DISPLAY

The advanced Heads-Up Display (HUD) makes every drive simpler and safer by projecting essential driving information directly onto the windshield allowing you to keep your eyes on the road and hands on the wheel, ensuring you stay focused on what matters most—your journey.

DM 5.0 & DM-POWERFUL TECHNOLOGY – MORE THAN INTELLIGENT

Thanks to the advanced DM-p Super-Hybrid system and intelligent decoupling technology, the Sealion 8 delivers remarkable combined fuel economy — as low as 1L/100 km.

By prioritising electric drive in everyday conditions and engaging the engine only when needed, it achieves powerful yet efficient performance for modern family life.

IMMERSIVE EXPERIENCE

The 15.6-inch touchscreen and 10.25-inch driver display put all your essentials within easy reach, while multiple USB-C ports and a wireless charger keep devices powered for the whole family — all supported by up to 2GB of complimentary in-car data per month for two years, 21-speaker premium audio, and multi colour gradient ambient lighting to set the mood.

Premium Interior

 

Step inside the luxurious Sealion 8 and you’ll find a cabin designed around modern family life — spacious, flexible, and high-tech. Whether it’s the school drop-off, weekend camping, or a night out in the city, the Sealion 8 keeps everyone comfortable and connected.

The Premium AWD model has genuine leather seats, massage functions, 21-speaker premium audio, and heated second-row seats — turning every drive into first-class family travel.

SAFETY FIRST

Nine airbags with curtains that extend to the third row, a body made of TRB hot-stamped steel for outstanding rigidity and protection, and the latest ADAS systems are all designed to keep you and your loved ones safe. A new internal RGB-and-IR camera system continously monitors the driver’s posture, seatbelt status and more.

VEHICLE TO LOAD & CHARGING

V2L functionality with discharge adaptor – allowing you to power a wide range appliances on the go. BYD SEALION 8 also features 11kW AC charging and 74kW DC charging.

Protection That Goes the Distance
